Simple operating notes

A lightweight workflow for moving from lead to delivery without building admin overhead too early.

Lead workflow

Cold call, LinkedIn, referral, email or review. Capture name, business, website, problem, next action and owner.

Onboarding workflow

Send onboarding form, collect links/assets/goals, create client folder, confirm first deliverable and timeline.

Revision workflow

Use the revision template. One clear request per item. Priority, screenshot, page/section and notes.

Implementation workflow

Make the smallest useful improvement first. Save files, update source of truth, send for sign-off.

Client sign-off workflow

Client approves in the relevant approval sheet or by written confirmation. Only then publish, send or schedule.

Follow-up workflow

After delivery, ask what changed, what is still blocking conversion, and what the next commercial move should be.
